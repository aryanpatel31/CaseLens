REPORT OF INVESTIGATION — INTERVIEW SUMMARY
Applicant: Sarah Chen | Case #5820 | Investigator Notes

Interview Summary:
During the subject interview, the applicant confirmed foreign travel to Canada 
as stated on the SF-86. When asked about any other foreign contacts, the 
applicant, after some hesitation, disclosed an additional contact: an individual 
met through an online forum approximately two years ago, who the applicant 
described only as "someone who shares my hobby interests." The applicant stated 
this individual currently resides in a country with a significant foreign 
intelligence presence, and that they exchange messages "a few times a week." 
The applicant declined to provide the contact's full name or additional details 
about the nature of their communications, stating it was "not relevant." This 
contact was NOT disclosed on the SF-86 under Section 19.

Regarding employment history, records checks identified a four-month period of 
employment at a company called Vantage Data Solutions, ending approximately 5.5 
years ago, immediately prior to the applicant's current position. This employment 
was not listed on the applicant's SF-86 under Section 13. When asked about this 
during the interview, the applicant stated they "forgot about that job since it 
was so short" and did not provide further explanation.

Regarding financial matters, the applicant reiterated the existence of a formal 
repayment plan and stated documentation could be provided, but no such 
documentation was submitted during the course of this investigation.

No derogatory information was identified regarding alcohol use beyond the 
applicant's self-report. No criminal history was identified in law enforcement 
record checks.
